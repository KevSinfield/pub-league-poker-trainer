Phases by Big Blinds

50-25 BB: Tight is right. Premium hands only.

25-12 BB: Steal selectively from late position.

Under 12 BB: Shove or fold.

Heads-up: Aggression wins.

Raise or Call?

First in: Raise or fold. Never just call (limp).

Vs a raise: Re-raise QQ+/AK, call small pair (set-mine), else fold.

BB vs raise: Defend wider - you already paid.
